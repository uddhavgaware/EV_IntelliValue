Platform Features

Everything You Need to Buy or Sell an EV Confidently

Four powerful modules built on machine learning and market data analysis.

Real-Time Valuation Predictor

Instant fair market price in ₹ INR based on brand, model, battery health, mileage, age and region. Low / Expected / High valuation bounds included.

  • Gradient Boosting model (R² = 0.976)
  • Dynamic price bounds for negotiation
  • Value driver breakdown by factor

Battery Degradation Lab

Non-linear SOH degradation curves across age and km. See exactly how each 1% battery drop impacts resale value in rupees and remaining range.

  • State-of-Health (SOH) modelling
  • Replacement cost risk warnings
  • Live sensitivity analysis curves

Market Intelligence Dashboard

Regional analytics for Pune, Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Chennai. Price distribution histograms, scatter plots, and days-on-market trends.

  • OLS trendlines on scatter plots
  • Brand market share analysis
  • Regional pricing benchmarks

Model Benchmark Dashboard

Live comparison of all three algorithms — Gradient Boosting, Random Forest, and Linear Regression — across MAE, RMSE, and R² metrics.

  • Feature importance ranking
  • Actual vs. predicted residual plots
  • Model selection explainability
Process

From Input to Instant Valuation

Three steps. Ten seconds. One reliable price estimate.

01

Enter Your EV Details

Select brand, model, manufacturing year, kilometres driven, battery capacity and current battery health percentage. Choose your city market.

02

ML Pipeline Processes

Your input runs through a trained Gradient Boosting pipeline — preprocessing, feature encoding, and inference — in under a second.

03

Get Your Price Estimate

Receive a fair market value in ₹ INR with low / expected / high bounds, key value driver breakdown, and comparable market listings.
